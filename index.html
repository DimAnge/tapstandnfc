TapStand panel customizer

Make a logo panel for the TapStand NFC stand. Everything runs in your browser; your files are not uploaded anywhere.
Logo No logo yet? Download the starter SVG, edit its text and upload it here.

Text Joined or right-to-left scripts (Arabic, Hindi…) are not shaped correctly; use a logo image for those.

How to print it

  1. 0.20 mm layers, 5 top layers, 4 bottom layers, 15% infill, no supports.
  2. Start with the body colour (white).
  3. Add a pause at 4.2 mm. In Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer: Preview, layer slider, right-click the +, Add Pause. In PrusaSlicer: Add color change.
  4. At the pause, switch to the detail colour (black), purge until clean, resume.
