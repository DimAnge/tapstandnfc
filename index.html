TapStand panel customizer

Make a logo panel for the TapStand NFC stand. Everything runs in your browser; your files are not uploaded anywhere.
Logo You can also drop an image anywhere on the page. No logo yet? or download the starter SVG, edit its text and upload it.

Text Joined or right-to-left scripts (Arabic, Hindi…) are not shaped correctly; use a logo image for those.

Colours
Shown in the preview and saved in the 3MF files as the two filament colours.
How to print it
  1. With an AMS: download 3MF for AMS, load the body colour in slot 1 and the detail colour in slot 2, slice and print. The change at 4.2 mm is already in the file.
  2. Without an AMS: download 3MF with pause. Start with the body colour; the printer stops at 4.2 mm. Switch to the detail colour, purge until clean, resume.
  3. Any other slicer: download the STL. Use 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 5 top and 4 bottom layers, 15% infill, no supports, and add a pause or colour change at 4.2 mm yourself (Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer: Preview, layer slider, right-click the +, Add Pause; PrusaSlicer: Add color change).

    The 3MF files open in Bambu Studio with the 4.2 mm colour change or pause already set. They carry an A1 mini profile; pick your own printer when asked.
